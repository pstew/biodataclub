# Hackathon Projects 2026

## Chatbot for BMT Caregivers

### Project Lead: [Christine Vinci](mailto:christine.vinci@moffitt.org)

**Background**: This hackathon project proposes to develop the initial version
of a chatbot that can be used by caregivers of BMT patients to ask questions
about patient care, such as hygienic precautions, and receive emotional support
resources for themselves, such as support groups. Although this information is
provided to caregivers throughout the treatment process, it is often fragmented
and easily forgotten.

**Project Significance**: Caregivers of BMT patients substantially contribute to
patient care through medication administration, appointment scheduling,
maintaining hygiene and food safety protocols, monitoring symptoms, and
providing emotional support. It is imperative that caregivers have quick, easy,
and reliable access to the information needed to support both patient care and
their own well-being.

**Technical Specifications or Programming Background**: Basic use of AI models
(e.g., ChatGPT or Claude), prompt engineering, and the ability to evaluate
generated responses against expected outcomes.

## History of Bioinformatics

### Project Lead: [Timothy Shaw](mailto:timothy.shaw@moffitt.org)

**Background**: Generate a GitHub-ready R Shiny application that guides visitors
through the evolution of bioinformatics, from early sequence analysis and
computational biology to genomics, single-cell sequencing, spatial omics, and
AI-driven biology.

**Project Significance**: The application will be designed for teaching,
seminars, and public outreach. It will feature an interactive historical
timeline, explanations of landmark discoveries, and links to foundational
publications.

**Technical Specifications or Programming Background**: GitHub, R, and Shiny
development.

## Moffitt Research Policy Library LLM

### Project Lead: [Alex Soupir](mailto:alex.soupir@moffitt.org)

**Background**: Finding specific guidance in Moffitt’s research policy library
can be frustrating because relevant documents may be scattered across multiple
sources. Traditional keyword searches can also miss critical context, especially
when policies overlap or reference one another. An agentic LLM could parse these
dense documents to provide clear answers backed by citations to the original
source text.

**Project Significance**: Searching through policies and documentation consumes
valuable research time and introduces compliance risks when conflicting or
outdated guidance is overlooked. Hosting a lightweight model locally within
Moffitt’s network can help maintain document security while providing immediate
answers to operational questions. Making institutional guidance easier to
discover and cross-reference can remove bottlenecks and improve consistency
across teams.

**Technical Specifications or Programming Background**: Access to MoffittNet for
policy retrieval, access to Cortex Code, project management skills, and
frontend/backend development experience.

## AI Model Applications for Biological Age Estimation and Survival Prediction

### Project Lead: [Dr. Shelly Li](mailto:shelly.li@moffitt.org) (co-lead with Dr. Gabriella Sanabria)

**Background**: Recent breakthroughs in deep learning have demonstrated that
facial photographs can serve as a non-invasive window into systemic health by
capturing subtle visual markers of biological aging and mortality risk.
Foundational models such as FAHR-Face use large-scale image representations to
support biological age estimation and cancer survival prediction. This project
aims to adapt these advanced algorithms into a scalable prototype application
that allows researchers to securely capture patient photos, age data, and
clinical outcomes to validate AI-driven facial biomarkers in a new institutional
setting.

**Project Significance**: Facial-based health recognition tools offer an
inexpensive, non-invasive, and low-friction method for risk stratification that
can supplement traditional clinical biomarkers. Building this application at
Moffitt will help bridge cutting-edge computer vision research and local
clinical translation, enabling rigorous validation across diverse patient
populations. The project lays the groundwork for improving prognostic accuracy,
supporting personalized cancer care, and advancing digital oncology research.

**Technical Specifications or Programming Background**: Required: Python
programming and GitHub experience.

Preferred: Experience with Hugging Face models and application development.

## Charity Gift Exchange

### Project Lead: [Richard Reich](mailto:richie.reich@moffitt.org)

**Background**: For many years, the BBSR and now multiple Data Science Shared
Resources have participated in a charity gift exchange each December. Volunteer
participants are randomly assigned to one another and make a donation to one of
the recipient’s favorite charities. This project proposes creating an
application that allows participants to register, indicate preferred charities,
perform the random assignment process, and integrate with Charity Navigator to
provide information about participating organizations.

**Project Significance**: Since 2020, the charity gift exchange has resulted in
more than \$3,000 in donations to nearly 100 charities. This application will
simplify administration of the exchange and help encourage continued
participation and charitable giving.

**Technical Specifications or Programming Background**: Creative problem-solving
and application design ideas that can be implemented using Cortex Code.
Experience with participant registration workflows, application deployment, and
server hosting would be beneficial.
